Our Services

Comprehensive Diagnostic Solutions for Your Health

Pathology

Our pathology department offers a wide range of tests including Hematology, Clinical Pathology, Cytopathology, and Histopathology. We use automated analyzers for precise results.

Biochemistry

We perform routine and specialized biochemistry tests such as Liver Function Tests (LFT), Kidney Function Tests (KFT), Lipid Profile, Diabetic Profile, and Cardiac Markers.

Microbiology

Our microbiology lab identifies infectious agents like bacteria, viruses, fungi, and parasites. We offer culture and sensitivity testing to guide appropriate antibiotic therapy.

Radiology & Imaging

Equipped with digital X-Ray, Ultrasound, and MRI facilities to provide high-quality imaging for accurate diagnosis of internal conditions.

Cardiology

We offer non-invasive cardiac diagnostic services including ECG, Echocardiography (Echo), and TMT (Treadmill Test) to assess heart health.

Health Packages

Comprehensive health check-up packages designed for different age groups and lifestyles, including Full Body Checkup, Women's Health, and Senior Citizen packages.

Book a Home Collection

Can't visit the lab? We bring the lab to your doorstep. Schedule a home sample collection at your convenience.
