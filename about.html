Our Journey

Max Path Lab began its journey with a clear focus on quality diagnostics and patient trust. Starting as a local diagnostic initiative, the laboratory has steadily grown by adopting modern technology, standardized testing procedures, and strict quality control systems. Over the years, Max Path Lab Agra has expanded its service portfolio and infrastructure to meet the growing diagnostic needs of the community, while maintaining accuracy, transparency, and clinical reliability at every step.
